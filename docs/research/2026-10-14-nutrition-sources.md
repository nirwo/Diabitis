## Safety flags (from fact-check)

1. Banana "ice cream" and cheese pancakes: sugar/starch → dumping risk; late stage only.
2. 60 g/day floor is low for OAGB; ERAS says ≥90 g. Target is set by the patient's dietitian.
3. Staging is a generic NHS protocol; the patient's own team overrides.
4. Higher-fat items may worsen diarrhoea after malabsorptive surgery — small portions.
5. Red meat and fibrous legumes: introduce late, small amounts.
6. Egg white: cooked only.
7. Lactose intolerance is common — lactose-free / isolate alternatives.
8. Use realistic portions (60–120 g early), not full tubs.
