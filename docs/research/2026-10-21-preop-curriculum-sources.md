# Pre-op dietitian preparation — verified sources (2026-10-21)

Two research agents gathered these; a separate fact-checker re-opened each source. Only CONFIRMED/CORRECTED items are listed.

## Israel

| Claim | Verdict | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ≥3 dietitian sessions, at most one every 2 weeks, over ≥6 weeks; summary letter to the pre-op committee (§5.4) | CONFIRMED | MoH circular 4/2024 — https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/mr04-2024/he/files_circulars_mr_mr04_2024.pdf |
| Annex 2 preparation components: adopting post-op eating & lifestyle habits; correcting nutritional deficiencies; pre-op weight loss; glucose control in diabetics; physical activity; knowledge & expectations; special populations | CONFIRMED | same |
| Clalit: ≥3 sessions over ≥6 weeks, letter at the end | CONFIRMED | https://www.clalit.co.il/he/myrights/surgeries/Pages/bariatric-surgery.aspx |
| Maccabi: 3–6 sessions in the 6 months before the committee, once every two weeks, first and last face to face (no "6 recommended") | CORRECTED | https://www.maccabi4u.co.il/eligibilites/11180/ |
| Maccabi leaflet habits to practise: regular meals, slow eating & chewing, separating eating from drinking, no carbonated drinks, more protein, physical activity | CONFIRMED | https://www.maccabi4u.co.il/media/eb3c3oqw/%D7%A2%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%97-%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%90%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%99.pdf |

No Israeli source gives a session-by-session topic order. No source requires a photo food diary.
